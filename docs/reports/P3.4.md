## Outcome and scope

Disagreements become findings with source values, timestamps, differences and a recommended action, and a person decides. The showcase is CRM customer versus NetSuite-style customer: one Harbor Ridge account in the CRM, two billable entities in the ERP Simulator. A reviewer approves the mapping, invoices keep their own bill-to, and a replay leaves the decision alone. All of it is simulated.

## Decisions and teaching notes

**Teach-back.** Business need: finance and sales stop arguing about whose list is right. Data path: stored source records and canonical rows go into a pure function, and only findings come out. Security boundary: approval applies a server-built resolution, and the only value from the request is a customer ID checked against the workspace. Failure: changed evidence refuses the approval. Tradeoff: a person handles each case, which is the requirement.
