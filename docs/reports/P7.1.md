## Security, quality and cost

- Trust boundaries: the request role can no longer claim or finish outbox events. The dispatcher sees one workspace's rules at a time and nothing else (`dispatch.test.ts`).
- Findings: one High (spike `ws`), fixed; one SAST hardening (report limit and offset), fixed; three undocumented routes, documented. No critical or high exploitable issue open.
- Licenses: runtime dependencies are MIT, ISC and Apache-2.0. LGPL-3.0 (`sharp`'s `libvips` binaries) and MPL-2.0 (`lightningcss`) are build tools only and don't ship.
- Cost: none. No account.
- Residual risk: the trivy database was a week old; SAST is a small rule set, not a general tool.

## Decisions and teaching notes

D-076 to D-078. **Plain language:** a scanner that never fails proves nothing, so each one was fed a planted problem first. An SBOM is the parts list of the software. A separate dispatcher role means a bug in a visitor's request can't touch the outbox at all, even with hand-written SQL.
