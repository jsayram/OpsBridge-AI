## Retention and deletion

- Demo workspaces: a 2-hour session; the sweep purges 5 expired workspaces every 30 minutes and writes a retention record. Even with all 150 expired at once, purge finishes in about 15 hours, inside PRD 12.3's 24 hours.
- **Residual risk, Medium (availability, not data):** expired workspaces count against the cap of 150 until purged, so a burst of launches from about 30 addresses could fill it and refuse new visitors for up to that 15 hours. Existing visitors are unaffected and no data is exposed. Options for P8: raise the purge batch once Workers CPU is measured on Cloudflare (each purge is one function call), lower the cap, or count only unexpired workspaces. Recorded, not fixed: changing the purge rate without a CPU measurement could break Workers Free.
- Backups: Neon's 6-hour restore window holds purged rows until it rolls over (`RECOVERY.md`). Not provisioned.
- Logs: request lines hold no cookie, token, body, query or address. Cloudflare log retention is a provider setting to confirm at deploy.
