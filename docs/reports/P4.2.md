## Outcome and scope

Committed events are now delivered. A command's response triggers one bounded dispatch pass for its workspace, and the 30-minute cron catches the rest, three events a tick. A claim takes a 130-second lease with a fresh token. `finish_outbox` refuses a stale token, so a worker whose lease was reclaimed can't overwrite the newer result. Every attempt writes a row to `outbox_attempts`. The effects ledger `automation_effects`, keyed by event and rule, makes a replay add nothing.

## Independent review

The review found the database didn't hold the demo-only-simulated rule by itself: the runtime role could finish a demo event as Accepted. `finish_outbox` now refuses that and checks every effect against a real rule (`89334a1`, D-061), with tests in `dispatch.test.ts`. `Retry-After` over an hour is now honored.

## Security, quality and cost

- Sink deduplication and uncertain delivery: the simulated sink's effect is the ledger row, so its primary key makes it exactly once. The sheet relies on check-before-append, which covers the tested failures but not an append Google applies more than 120 s late (the residual risk in `INTEGRATION-CONTRACTS.md`). The claim stays "no duplicate under the tested failures".
- The dispatcher doesn't write audit rows (D-056); attempts are its record. Operator actions still audit.
- No cost. The cron schedule is unchanged at `*/30`.
