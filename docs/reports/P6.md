## Phase result

The app now turns what it records into numbers a manager can act on, locally. `/app/analytics` shows operations pulse, the workflow funnel with a 90-day trend, integration health, data quality, AI assistance and automation. Every number is computed on request from the records the visitor can already see in the case list, carries a written formula, and is labeled Synthetic, Measured in demo, Estimated or Unknown. `/app/reports` filters cases by date, status, assignee and source and exports a CSV a spreadsheet can't execute, and the table, the per-status counts and the file always agree. `/app/roi` is a calculator over assumptions the visitor changes, labeled on every output as an illustrative projection using synthetic workload. `docs/EXECUTIVE-BUSINESS-CASE.md` is the one-page decision memo built from it.

**What is real and what isn't.** The records are fictional and the counts over them are exact. Every source is simulated (or, for the owner, a connected test org). The only measured figure is the time from an AI review request to its decision in the visitor's own session, kept out of the ROI. Every dollar figure is a projection from stated assumptions. Live AI cost shows as unknown, not zero, because live AI is still Pending Owner Credentials. Nothing is deployed.

## The business case in one paragraph

At 400 tasks a month, the base scenario projects about 53 hours and a net $1,800 a month, breaking even at 12 tasks. The conservative scenario loses $130 a month, because review plus exceptions take longer than the manual task. Net value depends most on manual minutes, labor rate, volume and review minutes, none of which has been measured. The memo recommends a two-week measured pilot and stopping if review isn't clearly faster than the manual task. All of it is illustrative.
