## Decisions and teaching notes

**Teach-back.** The restart proof doesn't pretend: it stops the real Worker, restarts the real database container, starts a new Worker and compares every field. The profile shows why cursors beat page numbers: row 900 costs what row 1 costs, because the database jumps straight there.
