## Outcome and scope

Done locally. `/app/reports` filters cases by UTC date range, status, assignee and source, sorts and pages. The heading count, the per-status table and the CSV come from one WHERE clause. Limits are on the page. Export is quoted CSV with formula-prefix cells neutralized and a Provenance column, refused over 1,000 rows rather than cut short. Viewers may export, as the P0.3 permissions matrix says (D-071).

## Security, quality and cost

- Every value is a bind parameter; ORDER BY comes from a closed map; LIMIT and OFFSET are integers checked before use.
- Logs record the export's row count only, never filters or rows.

## Independent review follow-up

- **UTC bounds (Medium, fixed).** `created_at >= $3::date` was read in the session's time zone. Bounds are now `::timestamp AT TIME ZONE 'UTC'`; a test sets the session to America/New_York and checks a 02:00 UTC case lands on its UTC day.
- **Silent export refusal (Medium, fixed).** Over the cap, the page replaces the link with a sentence giving the row count and the limit. The API still refuses with 413.
- **366 days with no `to` (Low, fixed).** "Today" is the UTC day, so an omitted `to` counts as a whole day; test added.
- **Leading spaces (Low, fixed).** A cell like ` =1+1` is neutralized too.
