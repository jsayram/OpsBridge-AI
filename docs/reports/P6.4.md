## Outcome and scope

Done locally. `/app/roi` implements the PRD 11.2 formulas line by line in `frontend/src/lib/roi.ts`, in the browser, storing nothing (D-074). Nine assumptions each show their source; the hourly rate is a placeholder. Conservative, base and optimistic presets, a side-by-side table, break-even volume, and a sensitivity table (each input 20% down and up). ROI is empty when modeled cost is zero. Outputs show two significant figures and carry the label "Illustrative projection using synthetic workload". Measured decision time from the demo sits in its own panel and is not used.

The conservative preset loses money (review plus exceptions take longer than the manual task). That is kept: it shows the answer depends on unmeasured minutes.
