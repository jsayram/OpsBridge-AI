## Phase result

The app now has a bounded, measurable AI review, on a local machine. A visitor attaches one of 40 fictional documents to a case and asks for a review. The answer is a structured suggestion in which every field points at the page and the exact text it came from, fields it couldn't find are listed as missing, and conflicting or unreadable input is marked uncertain or abstained. A manager then accepts, edits or rejects it. Only an accept changes the case, and only through the same version-checked checklist command a person uses, limited to what fixed rules allow from the cited fields.

**What is real and what isn't.** Every review a guest sees is a **Deterministic demonstration fixture**: answers produced at build time by a labeled-line extractor, stored by document hash, and replayed through the real validation path. No language model has been called. The owner's live path is built and tested against a labeled in-process double, so **P5.3 is Pending Owner Credentials** under the DEC-008 standing deferral. Under DEC-003 ("Stay free, fixtures only") there is no hosted upload, the Worker never parses a PDF, and the app stays on Workers Free at $0. Nothing is deployed.
