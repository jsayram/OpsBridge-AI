## Open item from P1

**Reset racing purge at the expiry boundary: confirmed real, and fixed.** An advisory lock parked a reset at the right moment, and Postgres killed the purge with `40P01`. The two took the same locks in opposite order. Purge now locks in reset's order and waits 60 seconds past expiry, and removing the fix makes a test fail (canary M11). Evidence: `docs/evidence/P2.0/`.
