## Security, quality and cost

- RLS, grants, narrow functions and an audit trigger now have negative tests behind them. Even the table owner can't rewrite an audit row.
- Local passwords are throwaway values in loopback-only config.
- No provider usage and no cost. `npm install` in the spike added dev-only packages listed in `spikes/p0.2/package.json`.
- Residual risk: a timed-out sheet append could land after the retry's check. See the threat model.
- The spike's `/pdf` route throws a bare 500 on bad input. That is proof code, and the app must map it to a typed failure.

## Decisions, teaching notes and approvals

**Teach-back.** Cloudflare's free plan allows 10 ms of CPU per request, and waiting on the database doesn't count. A normal request uses about 4 ms locally, so it fits. Parsing a PDF is pure CPU and uses about 2.5 ms a page, so anything past about three pages doesn't fit. The fix is to stop parsing on the public path: fixture documents get their text extracted once, at build time, and guests pick from those. Only owner uploads would parse live, and that decision can wait until P5.

DEC-003 is the question for Jose. Approval quote: none yet.
