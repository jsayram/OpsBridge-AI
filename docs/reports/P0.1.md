## Decisions, teaching notes and approvals

**Teach-back.** Think of this as a contract between the posting and the app. Each line says who can prove a requirement: the app, a document, or only Jose's real history. That stops a portfolio from drifting into claims it can't back up. The biggest tradeoff: people-leadership requirements are not faked with templates, so they stay interview material.
