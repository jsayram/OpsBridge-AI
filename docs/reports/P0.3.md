## Security, quality and cost

- Guests can't reach a live sink, and transport choice comes from the database, not the request.
- A leaked `opsbridge_app` credential could read all workspaces. Data is synthetic and it can't delete, and the risk is listed for owner review.
- Duplicate emails are prevented for tested failures, not in every conceivable case. The claim is written that way.
- No cost.

## Decisions, teaching notes and approvals

**Teach-back.** A Google Sheet append has no way to say "only do this once", so the app reads the ID column first. If the Worker dies after writing but before recording that it wrote, the retry finds its own row and stops. Separately, "the provider took it" and "a person got it" are now two different facts in two columns, which is why the word Delivered is gone. Mode A has no way to learn about the email, so an event stays Accepted unless Jose records proof.

Audit logs and demo cleanup conflicted, and the fix is narrow: one role, one trigger exception, expired demo workspaces only, with a log of each purge.
