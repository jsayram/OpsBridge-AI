## Outcome and scope

Done locally. `/app/analytics` shows operations pulse, workflow funnel with a 90-day trend, integration health, data quality, AI assistance and automation. Every metric card is a heading followed by its value, provenance badge and formula. Every chart is an SVG hidden from screen readers next to a table with the same numbers. Sources say Simulated with their last success time, or Never. Live AI cost says Not available. A manager or the owner can record today's snapshot.
