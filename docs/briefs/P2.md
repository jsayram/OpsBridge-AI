# P2 — Cases, canonical records and trustworthy activity history: phase brief

Status: planned, not started. Prepared at: 2026-11-06 America/New_York by the P1 phase session. PRD: v1.3, hash in `docs/DOCUMENT-SYNC.md`. Coordinator: master thread `local_2ce00b84-dd6c-4a69-9efd-3a293bde5688`.

## Goal and business reason

The app behaves as a real transactional business system, not a dashboard mock. A case moves through its states, an approval and its audit and outbox records commit together or not at all, and state survives a restart. Proves P2-A to P2-E, JOB-04, JOB-10.

**Plain language.** P1 proved who can see what. P2 proves that what they do is recorded correctly, even when two people click at once or the database fails halfway.

## Must carry forward from P1

- Treat migration `0001` as frozen from now on. New work goes in `0002` onward, and a changed checksum stops the runner.
- Demo limits live in `app.demo_limits`, so change them with a migration, never a Worker variable.
- Every new business table needs RLS on, write policies through `app.writable_workspace()`, a composite foreign key, and an entry in `purge_demo_workspace`. The grant test in `tests/integration/migrations.test.ts` fails on any unlisted grant, which is the point.
- Add new tables to the purge in dependency order, and extend the sweep test.
- `withWorkspace` is the only way to run workspace-scoped SQL. Use `set_config(..., true)` in an explicit transaction, never in a CTE.
- Reset cases: `pack_key` copies rows. Any table in the pack needs its own copy step in `seed_demo_workspace`, through a new migration replacing that function.
- Add an audit row to any new write. `hashHex` covers before and after summaries.
- Decide at P2.1 whether Drizzle earns its place (D-014).
- Browser runs share one owner row, so tests that revoke it run once per server.
