## Goal and business reason

A provider-neutral review contract, and a deterministic fixture provider that answers it, so guests always get the same labeled output and every claim cites a real segment. Proves P5-A, P5-B, part of P5-C, and GOAL-06.

**Plain language.** Whatever produces a suggestion, fixture or model, its answer is treated as untrusted text. It must parse as the exact JSON shape, use only known fields, cite segment IDs that exist in that document, and quote values that actually appear in the cited text. Anything else is rejected, never repaired. Business rules, not the suggestion, decide which checklist changes are allowed.
