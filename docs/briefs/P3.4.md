## Goal and business reason

Turn each disagreement into a finding with the source values attached, and let a person decide. The showcase is CRM customer versus NetSuite-style customer. Proves P3-A, P3-F, P3-H, JOB-20 and JOB-TEST-01.

**Plain language.** Harbor Ridge is one organization to sales and two billable entities to finance. Both are right. The system shows why, a reviewer approves a mapping once, and a replay leaves the decision alone.
