## Goal and business reason

Deliver every committed event at least once, and make a repeat harmless. Proves P4-B and P4-E, JOB-24 in part.

**Plain language.** The dispatcher borrows an event for 130 seconds (a lease). If it dies, the lease runs out and another pass picks the event up. Each borrow gets a fresh token, so an old worker can't overwrite a newer result. Retries wait at least two minutes and back off to an hour; after six tries the event is Failed and waits for a person.
