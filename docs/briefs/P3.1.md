## Goal and business reason

Say exactly what each source sends and what each word means, before any data moves. Proves JOB-03, JOB-20 and part of P3-F and P3-H.

**Plain language.** An adapter contract is a promise about shape. A failure class decides whether a problem deserves a retry. The glossary records that "customer" means something different in the CRM and the ERP, which is the heart of this phase.
