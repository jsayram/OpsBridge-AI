## Goal and business reason

Turn the five separate demo screens into one guided path a first-time interviewer can finish in five minutes: a checklist that marks steps done from the records, a recap that separates happened, simulated and measured, a reset, a keyboard path and a fallback when anything is offline. Proves P8-A (locally, up to the human timing) and P8-E. Also explain the WebKit "New case" intermittent from P7.4 if the cause is findable locally.

**Plain language.** The visitor shouldn't need a guide in the room. The page tells them what to do next and shows what they did, and it can't claim a step they didn't do because it reads the database.

## Scope

- In scope: `GET /api/v1/demo/progress` (guest only, read-only, documented in OpenAPI so the negative sweep covers it), the `/demo` checklist, recap and reset, a menu link back to it, a Manager-role shortcut, the offline fallback, `docs/DEMO-SCRIPT.md` with the video shot list, a timed automated run in Chromium and WebKit.
- Non-goals: recording or publishing a video, a real person's timed run, new business features.
