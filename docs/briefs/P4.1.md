## Goal and business reason

Let a team describe "when this happens, do that" without code, and make sure no rule can ever point somewhere a visitor chose. Proves P4-D in part, JOB-12, JOB-18 and JOB-TEST-06.

**Plain language.** A rule is a trigger from a closed list, a condition built only from listed fields and values, and one of two actions. Because a rule has no field for an address, link or code, there is nothing to smuggle a destination into. Reuse means a second fictional team gets the same rule pointed at its own cases, served by the same evaluator.

## Scope and prerequisites
