## Goal and business reason

Give every review a real, fixed input: 40 fictional PDFs whose text was extracted once at build time, stored as page-numbered segments with a hash. Proves the P5.1 row as DEC-003 reshaped it, and E2E-13.

**Plain language.** A PDF is never opened by the app at request time. A script makes the 40 PDFs, reads their text in Node, and saves the text split into small numbered pieces ("segments"). The app reviews those pieces. Because each piece has an ID and a page, a suggestion can point at exactly where a value came from. An owner upload route exists only to prove that bad files are refused before anything happens to them; it then says uploads are off, because Jose chose fixtures only.
