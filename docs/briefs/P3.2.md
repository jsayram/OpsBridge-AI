## Goal and business reason

Bring simulated CRM and ERP data in through the same pipeline a real source would use, and prove a replay is harmless. Proves P3-B and JOB-19. The ERP Simulator follows documented SuiteTalk REST conventions because the role names NetSuite.

**Plain language.** A cursor is a bookmark. The bookmark only moves after the page it follows is saved, so a crash can repeat a page but can never skip one.
