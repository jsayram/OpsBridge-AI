## Goal and business reason

Show each event's state truthfully and give the owner the controls an operator needs: retry, record evidence, stop everything. Proves P4-D and JOB-24's distinction between simulated, queued, accepted, confirmed and failed.

**Plain language.** "The provider took it" and "a person got it" are different facts. A 200 from Google only reaches Accepted. Confirmed needs the owner's Zap run reference. The emergency stop holds every event where it is, so nothing is lost while it's on.
