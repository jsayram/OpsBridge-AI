# P8 — Interview-ready public launch and ownership handoff: phase brief

Status: planned, not started. Prepared at: 2026-11-09 America/New_York by the P7 phase session. PRD: v1.4, SHA-256 `95b85b6a6e817d9aabdadf8534f3cb7a0e196ec0408e733c6771752ef2ef5785`. Coordinator: master thread `local_2ce00b84-dd6c-4a69-9efd-3a293bde5688`.

## Goal and business reason

Turn the verified local release into something a hiring manager can open, use for five minutes and check against its evidence, and hand Jose everything he needs to run, update and shut it down himself. Proves P8-A to P8-G.

**Plain language.** P0 to P7 built and checked the app on Jose's machine. P8 puts it on the internet only after Jose creates the accounts and says yes, records a short demo, writes the case study and the "start here" guide, and checks every public sentence against what the tests actually showed.

- P7 reviewed by the master and recorded in the ledger. P7 left these pending, and P8 depends on them: staging deploy and smoke (P1-E), Hyperdrive, Neon, Access and R2 proofs, Workers Free CPU per request, hosted restore and rollback (P7-C hosted half), a VoiceOver pass by a person (P6-E, P7-D), real Safari on a Mac and an iPhone.
- **Owner steps before any hosted work** are listed in `docs/reports/P7.md` under "Release readiness". Most of P8.4 can't start until Jose has done the staging half of that list.
- Live-account criteria (P5.3, P4-C, P3-D) stay Pending Owner Credentials under DEC-008 unless Jose sets them up. The public demo must work, and say so, without them (P8-E).

## Stop points

Accounts, logins, credentials, paid or hosted anything, domains, deploys, GitHub repo creation, pushes, merges into `main`, external sends, real data, permanent deletions, a PRD 12.4 security release blocker, a failed ADR-000 proof, and material scope changes. Publication of any text, video or URL is a stop point. Keep building whatever does not depend on a blocked item. DEC-008 is a standing deferral for live-account criteria.
