## Goal and business reason

Check the critical paths in Safari's engine and on an iPhone-sized screen as well as Chrome, and measure a small realistic load. Proves P7-D locally and the PRD 13.2 latency target on local hardware.

**Plain language.** Playwright can drive WebKit, the engine inside Safari, on this Mac. It is close to Safari but not Safari itself, so the report says so. The load profile runs twenty simulated visitors clicking through the demo for a minute and records how long each kind of request took.
