## Goal and business reason

Every number a manager sees comes from one written formula over the caller's own rows, with its provenance, and reconciles with the transactional tables after any change. Proves P6-A and the P6 part of JOB-04.

**Plain language.** A metric glossary, a per-request overview over the same rows the case list shows, a snapshot table for trends, and query plans read at seeded volume before any index.
