## Goal and business reason

Bring in read-only stock facts on the same pipeline, and let a consultant see source context on a case. Proves P3-G, JOB-11 and JOB-TEST-03.

**Plain language.** A quantity is a fact a source reported at a moment. OpsBridge keeps it and shows its age. It never edits it, converts its unit or invents an item for it.
