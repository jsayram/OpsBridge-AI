## Goal and business reason

Try every operation as the wrong caller and take each provider away, and show nothing leaks, nothing unauthorized is written and the demo keeps working. Proves P7-B and P7-E.

**Plain language.** One test reads the API description and calls every operation as nobody, as a guest holding forged owner tokens, from another website, with another visitor's IDs and as a read-only viewer, and checks the other visitor's data is byte-for-byte unchanged. Another switches off the AI provider, Salesforce, the Sheet bridge and the identity provider in turn and walks the visitor's whole path.
