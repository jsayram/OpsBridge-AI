## Goal and business reason

Verify the whole system is safe, resilient and maintainable before anyone shares it widely. No new product features. Proves P7-A to P7-F and the P7 parts of `docs/JOB-ALIGNMENT.md`.

**Plain language.** P7 tries to break what P0 to P6 built, rehearses getting it back after a failure, checks it in more than one browser and with a screen reader, and writes down honestly what was and wasn't proven. Anything that needs a hosted environment stays Pending Owner Credentials/Configuration until Jose creates the accounts.
