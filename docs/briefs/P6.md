Status: planned, not started. Prepared at: 2026-11-09 America/New_York by the P5 phase session. PRD: v1.4, SHA-256 `95b85b6a6e817d9aabdadf8534f3cb7a0e196ec0408e733c6771752ef2ef5785`. Coordinator: master thread `local_2ce00b84-dd6c-4a69-9efd-3a293bde5688`.

## Goal and business reason

Turn what the app records into numbers a manager can act on, each with its source and freshness, and a ROI calculator that never pretends to be a real saving. Proves P6-A to P6-G, and the P6 parts of JOB-04, JOB-08, JOB-11, JOB-12, JOB-17, JOB-20, JOB-21 and JOB-22 in `docs/JOB-ALIGNMENT.md`.

**Plain language.** Every number on a dashboard comes from one written formula over the workspace's own records, and a test recomputes it from the seed. A card says whether its source is simulated, measured or estimated, and how old it is. The ROI page is a calculator over assumptions the visitor can change; it is always labeled an illustrative projection using synthetic workload (PRD 11.2).
