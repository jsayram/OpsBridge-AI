## Goal and business reason

A manager filters cases by date, status, assignee and source, pages through them and exports a CSV that a spreadsheet can't execute, with table, chart and file always agreeing. Proves P6-F, JOB-21 and JOB-TEST-04.

**Plain language.** Bounded report and export routes sharing one WHERE clause, documented limits, formula neutralization, and refusal of unscoped, oversize and unknown requests.
